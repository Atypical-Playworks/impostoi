# Pseudonymous play and persistent progress

Status: Accepted

Players may join as Guests or authenticated Supabase users. A Match always shows a temporary Alias and Avatar, regardless of persistent account status.

Guest play has no mandatory registration. A Guest can migrate eligible history to a persistent account once. Persistent accounts can view personal detection statistics and future leaderboards.

Detailed Replays are visible to Match Participants and expire after the product retention period. Public views expose anonymized summaries and aggregate Agent statistics only.
