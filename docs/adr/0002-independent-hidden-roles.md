# Independent hidden roles

Status: Accepted

Each Round has exactly one Agent and exactly one Impostor. The Agent may be the Impostor or may be a Civilian while a human Player is the Impostor. The visible Alias never reveals either role.

The public Category is visible to every Participant. Civilians receive the Secret Word privately. The Impostor receives only the Category. The Agent receives the information allowed by its assigned role and always attempts to hide its AI identity.

The MVP assigns the Agent role and Impostor role independently with a balanced probability across Rounds.
